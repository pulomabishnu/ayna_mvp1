Delete your ayna account and data

ayna is operated by Ayna Health Inc. This page explains how users can request deletion of an ayna account and the personal data associated with it.

Option 1: Delete your account in ayna

  1. Sign in to your ayna account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Account or the account privacy controls.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete the account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access your account, or you want help with a deletion request, email us from the email address associated with your ayna account and use the subject line Account and Data Deletion Request.

Request data deletion

We may need to verify that you are the account owner before completing a manual request.

Data deleted with an account deletion

When an account deletion is completed, ayna deletes the active account identity and active user-linked personal data handled by the account deletion flow. Depending on which features you used, this can include:

  • account information such as name, email address, phone number, authentication identity, and account preferences;
  • health intake and health profile information you chose to save, including symptoms, concerns, conditions, medications, allergies, cycle or life-stage information, and related profile answers;
  • saved ecosystem, product, tracking, review, preference, and recommendation-related account records;
  • notification and phone-verification records stored with your account;
  • user-linked feedback and support information stored by ayna where it is connected to your account or email;
  • other active user-owned records that are configured to be removed when the authentication account is deleted.

Data that may be kept

ayna may retain limited records when retention is required or permitted for legal compliance, security, fraud or abuse prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational purposes. Service providers may also retain limited backup, security, or system-log copies for their normal retention periods before those copies age out.

We do not use account deletion as a way to keep active profile data. Any exceptional retained information is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the applicable legal, security, or operational purpose.

Retention after deletion

ayna does not currently promise one fixed retention period for every exceptional record or service-provider backup. Active account-linked records handled by the deletion flow are removed when deletion completes. Any limited records that must be retained are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary or legally required for the purpose described above.

Delete specific data without deleting your account

You may also contact us to request deletion or correction of specific personal or consumer health data without deleting the entire account, where applicable. Email puloma@aynahealth.co and describe the data you want deleted.

For more information, see the ayna Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice.
