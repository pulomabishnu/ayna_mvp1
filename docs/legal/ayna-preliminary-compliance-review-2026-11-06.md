# ayna preliminary US legal/compliance review (Nov 6, 2026)
**Status: research and code-spot-check only. NOT a legal opinion, certification, or production sign-off.**
**Scope:** personalized health-product discovery/matching, Ask ayna/AI, affiliate recommendations, connected health data, Pro subscriptions and community crowdfunding. Non-US legal analysis is outside this review. All URLs below are official regulators/statutes except when explicitly stated.

## P0: claims, evidence and potential FDA device status
* US FTC: health benefit, safety, efficacy and "works for you" claims, INCLUDING their implied meaning, need competent and reliable scientific substantiation; seller-provided marketing copy alone is not substantiation. Assess *full evidence*, not cherry-picked positive studies. [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance).
* FDA Jan 2026 CDS guidance: statutory non-device clinical decision support exclusion is principally for health-care-professional recommendations where users can independently evaluate basis; patient-facing symptom assessment and disease-focused directives need function-by-function FDA software review. A disclaimer cannot change the objective intended use. [FDA CDS Jan 2026](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software).
* FDA Jan 2026 general wellness policy: low-risk functions unrelated to diagnosing, treating, preventing or mitigating disease are lower risk, but this policy has limits and doesn't cover drugs or dietary supplements. [FDA general wellness](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices).
* Dietary supplement disease treatment/prevention/cure claims can change the product's regulatory status; do not turn manufacturer language into ayna's own medically established assertion, especially in matching explanations or landing-page copy. [FDA dietary supplements Q&A](https://www.fda.gov/food/information-consumers-using-dietary-supplements/questions-and-answers-dietary-supplements).
* `NN% match` must NOT imply "NN% chance of improving your health", medical efficacy, FDA clearance, or clinical safety. Keep explanations of the actual heuristic adjacent to the label and test user comprehension.
* Clinical opinions / medical evidence must be categorized explicitly: clinician *personally reviewed this exact product* vs published *study of formulation* vs published *ingredient-level* evidence vs manufacturer claim. Don't attribute endorsement to a clinician who has not reviewed the actual SKU.
* The phrase "not medical advice" does NOT remove duty to substantiate claims, prevent misleading implied claims, or follow applicable FDA regulations. **Flag "ayna fixes hormones"/"ayna can cure" marketing copy** rather than assuming humor makes it safe. Avoid claims that ayna can normalize hormones, diagnose infection or treat medical conditions.
* **Decision policy:** inconclusive trials, lack of studies, evidence only for ingredient, or a generic high-dose recommendation can give weaker evidence weight and contextual warnings; do not assert a treatment benefit without adequate substantiation. Explicit label contraindications, known serious interactions for the actual reported medicine, pregnancy restrictions and recalled products need a safety exclusion/hold. A warning does not override a known contraindication.

## P0: data privacy, wearables, AI, analytics
* The [FTC Health Breach Notification Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0) can apply to non-HIPAA apps that can aggregate health information from multiple sources. Unauthorized sharing can trigger notifications, not only a hack. Assess whether ayna is a PHR vendor/related entity and create an incident playbook.
* [FTC Premom fertility-app action](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/202-3186-easy-healthcare-corporation-us-v) specifically concerned fertility/period/pregnancy activity and analytics recipients. Product event names, stable IDs, product/condition categories, and URLs may reveal sensitive health data even with free text removed. Test **real outgoing analytics payloads**, not only `src/utils/analyticsSanitize.js` unit tests. Never send health profile, symptom/event payloads, cycle data, wearable metrics, records, personal IDs, or health-derived ad segments to marketing analytics without applicable lawful basis/consent.
* Washington [My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373.030](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=19.373.030) can require specified-purpose consent to collect and **separate consent to share** consumer health data, except applicable necessary-service exceptions. [Nevada NRS 603A.500](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-603A.html) likewise regulates health data. Confirm applicability to online users in those states; do not assume NYC incorporation exempts ayna.
* [NY Health Information Privacy Act S.9269](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9269) was **passed both houses** as of the official legislative tracker accessed Nov 6, 2026; **do not treat it as enacted** without verifying signature/state law. Continue monitoring.
* Existing repo file `docs/legal/consumer-health-data-policy-DRAFT.md` expressly says NOT approved/not live and shows consent/processor TODOs. **Do not release new wearable/records/AI data transfers until published notices accurately describe actual recipients, category permissions, retention/deletion, and vendor contracts/DPA.** Verify Apple's HealthKit and Android Health Connect platform terms separately.
* Inspect current PostHog default-on behavior, GPC handling, pseudonymous distinct IDs and any health-event naming. Code-level sanitization is a good start, not a verified compliance result.

## P1: affiliate/product ranking and commerce
* FTC endorsement guidance: material affiliate and brand relationships must be **clearly and conspicuously** disclosed near relevant links/recommendations, not buried in an About or Terms page. [FTC endorsement FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).
* Repo `src/components/HowWeMakeMoney.jsx` states partner visibility may affect **general Browse**, but not personalized matching, and includes the Amazon Associates disclosure. Audit each Shop, homepage, mobile card and checkout surface for prominent nearby disclosure and actual ranking consistency. Don't suggest partners are "clinically preferred" due to payment.
* Product labels, prices, stock and attribution must be accurate and current. Separate manufacturer claim from vetted sourced evidence in any generated product content.

## P1: ayna Pro recurring billing
* [New York GBL §527-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/527-A) requires clear renewal terms, affirmative consent, retainable acknowledgment, accessible cancellation, and specified notices. NYC has additional rules effective **Oct 1, 2026** [NYC automatic renewal rules](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCrules/0-0-0-155830).
* Audit the exact early-access deal: $4.99 monthly/$45 yearly charged now, immediate Pro access, launch term begins Feb 1, 2027, and later standard renewal of $8.99 monthly/$90 yearly. Verify any actual checkout copy, first/next charge dates, auto-renew consent, cancellation method, refund policy and renewal reminders against real Stripe settings. This document is NOT proof checkout matches copy.

## P1: Wefunder / Regulation Crowdfunding
* [SEC Reg CF Rule 204 guidance](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/regulation-crowdfunding) limits ads *outside the portal* that contain terms of an active Reg CF offering, including the security type, price, offering amount and funding progress. A compliant notice can link to Wefunder but may include only enumerated offering terms and limited factual issuer information. A social post that mixes funding terms with investment pitches, traction claims and founder narrative needs review under Rule 204.
* Confirm whether the Wefunder round is pre-filing **testing the waters**, an actual filed Form C/active Reg CF offering, or another exemption: different communications requirements apply. Do not assume published Wefunder page means securities can be sold now.
* Ask securities counsel/intermediary to approve reusable off-platform fundraising notice templates before further promotion.
