## Important unresolved legal / operational release blockers
1. **Production release:** The code/UI fixes are in **draft PR #132**, not main, public website, or TestFlight. The live payment-term DB date is corrected but the live page is not. Keep consistent copy and backend before taking additional money. An attempted separate hotfix branch creation was blocked by connected GitHub safety checks, so no production hotfix was merged.
2. **Annual renewal notices:** NYC rules require a notice for qualifying annual renewals **15–45 days** before the cancellation deadline; other trial and price-change reminders may apply. A working recurring reminder/send workflow and proof of delivery have **not** been verified. [NYC auto-renewal rules](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCrules/0-0-0-155830).
3. **NY auto-renew consent/checkout:** Verify actual Stripe Checkout terms in live browser, affirmative consent, receipts, cancel steps on both devices, any tax obligations, and treatment of charge failures. Source tests do not verify visual proximity/checkout experience. [NYC rules](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCrules/0-0-0-155830).
4. **FTC product and ad claims:** Source-level audit of all 152 catalog metadata gaps, model-generated text, website/landing/flyer/email ads and 38 flagged product reviews is incomplete. Confirm each benefit claim is appropriately supported and manufacturer text is labeled; don't conflate ingredient research with product-level efficacy. [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance).
5. **Clinical opinion terminology:** An ayna catalog 'clinical opinion' is cited published evidence/commentary, NOT legally required clinician sign-off. However internal `needs_clinician_review` flags also signify serious drug-dose, interaction or missing safety facts. The draft still conservatively holds all flagged products pending a per-SKU split into evidence caveat vs actual safety hold. Do NOT wholesale disable existing safety protection. [NIH iron 45 mg UL including exceptions](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/iron-HealthProfessional/).
6. **Device-law determination:** Patient-facing AI and matching intended use can trigger FDA scrutiny based on behavior/claims. A qualified US regulatory counsel assessment per function is outstanding. [FDA Jan2026 CDS](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software).
7. **Consumer-health privacy:** Verify Washington/Nevada and other applicable state consent requirements, health data recipient map, vendor DPAs, actual outbound event/network payloads, wearable SDK consent, deletion and breach workflows. Text edits alone do not prove compliance. [FTC Health Breach Notification Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0).
8. **Apple iOS storefront:** US App Store allows external digital-payment links in current guidelines; other storefronts generally have restrictions/entitlement requirements. Current native paywall link to the web requires storefront-specific review before international iOS distribution. [Apple App Review 3.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
9. **SEC crowdfunding:** Wefunder "testing the waters" vs filed active Reg CF status must be checked before posting investment-terms promotions off-platform. Only appropriate Rule 204 notices should contain terms; securities counsel/intermediary approval needed. [SEC Reg CF](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/regulation-crowdfunding).
10. **FTC affiliate visibility:** Nearby disclosure text exists in main product screens; verify all routes and links visually, and that paid Browse placements cannot masquerade as personalized recommendations. [FTC endorsements FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).
