# Matching engine audit, Oct 29 2026 (P0 step 1)

Scope: every path that turns a user profile and a catalog product into a
recommendation or a Match %. Branch `matching/p0-safety-foundation` (from main
46614f26 + the You page fix).

## How a match is made today

```
quiz / health profile ──> buildUserProfile (userProfile.js)
catalog product ────────> hydrateCatalogProduct ─> buildProductProfile (productProfile.js, + passport)
                                     │
                                     ▼
 scoreProductV2 (v2.js) ─> scoreProduct (score.js) ─> scoreProductForNeed ─> evaluateSafety
                                     │
                                     ▼
 buildMatchDetails (data/products.js) ─> percent | safety-hold | excluded | no-profile
 buildEcosystemV2 (v2.js) ───────────────> My Ecosystem shelves (+ v1 fallback picks)
 rankCatalog / For you / Ask / Compare / Rundown / PDP read buildMatchDetails
```

Every surface goes through `evaluateSafety`, so it is the single place where a
central gate can sit. That part of the design is sound.

Already right: Oura, Garmin and Strava data never create needs
(`src/health/policy.js`, `matchContext.js` corroborate only). No partner,
price or commission field is read by eligibility. A "safety-hold" state already
exists (no percent, one reason, one fix) and every screen renders it.

## What "hold" means after this branch

A hold is never a low %. It is no % plus one plain reason and one next step:

| State | Shown as | When | Her next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| needs_answer | "We need one answer" | Her profile is missing a fact this product depends on | Opens that question |
| confirm | "Please confirm your answers" | Two of her answers conflict on safety, or a safety answer is old | Opens the question |
| researching | "Checking this product" | We don't have a product fact her profile makes matter | Nothing |
| in_review | "Match being reviewed" | A clinician review is open on this product | Nothing |
| verified_unknown | "The brand doesn't share this" | We looked; the brand does not publish it | Ask a pharmacist |

Excluded (her stage, her allergy, a contraindication) stays excluded with the
reason, as before.
