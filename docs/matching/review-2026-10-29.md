# Algorithm review, Oct 29 (second pass)

Method: 160 seeded personas (all life stages and two conflicting pairs,
allergies, listed medicines, "not sure" medicines, pregnancy trimesters,
breastfeeding) x 281 products x every surface (product page details, the
gate, For you, top match, ecosystem seed, shelves by need), plus age-swap and
add-an-allergy perturbations. Then the 250-persona sweep against pre-P0 main
(46614f26), v1 vs v2, and list calibration (Steck 2018, KL between need
weights and shelf mix).

## Safety invariants: 0 violations
Scored products never: contain her allergen, are contraindicated in her
pregnancy, are cycle trackers without a cycle, are stage-scoped outside her
stage, have an open clinician review. Held products never show a percent.
The gate and every surface agree. Age never changes a match (no stage
inferred from age). For you, top match and every shelf hold only scored
products.

## Bugs found and fixed
1. "Strong fit" shown at 80+ when the engine's band was Good (low
   confidence, e.g. medicines "not sure"): 92 of 2,432 scored pairs. The word
   now follows the band.
2. Product page explainer dropped the new safety checks and evidence grade
   (decision.js did not pass them through).
3. Post-menopause: no quiz topic and no Shop life-stage filter. Added both;
   post-menopause personas get no period, cycle-tracking or prenatal picks.
4. Quiz copy: "How often?" had no subject; the age info implied age decides
   life stage (it never does).

## Not bugs (by design, documented)
- Adding an allergy can raise a percent by 1 to 2 points when the product is
  confirmed free of it (it fits her better).
- One telehealth service (Pomelo Care) appears in about a third of
  ecosystems because many products are on hold; it fills shelves that would
  otherwise be empty (often prenatal and lactation shelves). The fix is
  research (npm run research:plan), not lowering the bar.

## Compared with pre-P0 main (quiz safety questions answered)
coverage 0.37 -> 0.28, empty shelves 0.23 -> 0.33, avg picks 3.5 -> 2.7,
strong share 0.30 -> 0.30, category diversity 0.83 -> 0.87. The drop is the
cost of "unknown is not safe": held products leave the shelves. v1 vs v2:
v2 shows fewer Strong labels (0.12 vs 0.25 on the raw sweep) and more
varied categories per ecosystem (0.86 vs 0.70).
